## Limits and next decision

The causal intervention is local-rule behavior conditional on the two existing dense-trained parents. The factorial does not compare equally trained methods, generalize over training seeds/maps, establish new biological learning rules, or reproduce a complete Titans model. Bulk decay and erasure change arithmetic; persistent fast state, retained module writes, data access and routing budgets match, while FLOPs do not. The short task contains one conflicting revision; longer revision sequences and new codebooks remain outside this scope.

Matched training remains necessary even after this positive frozen diagnostic. EMA may learn to repair its own revision behavior and approach a ceiling under the fresh shared training protocol. In that case, failure of primary superiority is a valid result even if both methods qualify as reusable baselines. No rule, map, margin or gate was changed based on these frozen results.
