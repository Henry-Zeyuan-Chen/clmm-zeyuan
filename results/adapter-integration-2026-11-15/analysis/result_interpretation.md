# Reference adapter integration: independent results

The reference sleep and associative-memory adapters execute under the shared literal codebook, causal streams, state API and saved evaluation contract. The prespecified development-pilot result is a **selective-access benefit within the Hebbian systems**, not a demonstrated advantage of the Hebbian rule. At K8,D64 online stable recall, top-one Hebbian accuracy is 21.48% versus 5.47% for dense Hebbian: **+16.02 percentage points, paired 95% interval [8.98,23.44]**. Both systems pass their own K8 admission gates. The matched gradient systems gain even more from selective access, and gradient top-one outperforms independently trained Hebbian top-one.

These are explicitly reference component implementations because the refreshed team repository had no uploaded sleep/Hebbian code beyond empty directories. They are not a teammate's final algorithm, a full Titans/MAC model, a completed paper-style baseline, or a novelty claim.

## What ran and what was controlled

Frozen source: `a41bb097af3e7eed356d13e24e4a0d5bc6677b14`. The prospective design and launcher hashes are in `prospective_freeze_review.json`. A GPU throughput probe measured actual B16 pressure batches; its time-only projection passed the declared limit. All 19 invocations completed with no runtime skips: one engineering probe, eight trained associative systems, two descriptive pretrained neural anchors and eight fixed-slow-weight forks. No configuration or seed was selected by score.

The four associative systems have identical parameter geometry, literal key/value vectors, initial slow tensor bits within each seed, training arrays, 1,200-update endpoints and optimizer budgets. They differ in update rule (analytic associative gradient or Hebbian EMA) and access (all four modules or top one). Every system has 81,921 trainable slow elements and 64 KiB of adaptive trace tensors per stream. Top-one changes **both read and write access**, and inactive traces receive no decay. Dense and top-one have four versus one logical active-module updates per observed/replayed record; the vectorized implementation still computes dense candidate tensors, so this is not a fourfold compute or speed claim.

Representations, alpha and eta are independently trained and diverge across systems. Their main comparison therefore concerns equally budgeted trained systems, not an isolated online-rule intervention. The neural anchor uses a different architecture, fewer slow parameters and different live-state storage; it is descriptive rather than resource-matched.

## Prespecified primary and the rule/access distinction

K8,D64 online stable recall uses two stable queries in each of 64 shared histories:

| Hebbian initializer | Dense | Top-one | Top-one minus dense, percentage points [95% interval] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 90511 | 7/128,5.47% | 26/128,20.31% | 14.84 [7.81,22.66] |
| 90515 | 7/128,5.47% | 29/128,22.66% | 17.19 [9.38,25.00] |
| Mean of these two fixed initializers | 5.47% | 21.48% | 16.02 [8.98,23.44] |

Bootstrap resampling uses the same 64 whole history IDs across both seeds and conditions, 5,000 draws, seed 90604. The interval describes sampled-history uncertainty for two fixed checkpoints and one codebook; the seed×query records are not independent training replications. The design labels this a paired development pilot with unadjusted intervals, requiring later fresh confirmation for a broader efficacy claim.

Secondary trained-system means at that same condition are gradient dense 6.25%, gradient top-one 36.72%, Hebbian dense 5.47%, and Hebbian top-one 21.48%. Gradient top-one minus dense is +30.47 points [21.09,40.23]. Hebbian minus gradient under top-one is **−15.23 points [−22.27,−8.59]**; under dense access it is −0.78 points [−3.52,1.17]. Thus selective access helps both rules in this pilot, and these results do not show a superior Hebbian rule. The neural anchor also averages 36.72% at this condition, a coincident point estimate that does not establish equivalence or an architecture-matched result.

## Fixed-slow-weight forks

For each trained gradient/dense parent, all slow tensor values were copied into each rule/access configuration, traces reset to zero, and no further training performed. These planned forks remove independently learned representation/eta differences, conditional on a gradient-trained parent, while retaining the combined read/write intervention:

| Frozen-transfer configuration | Online K8,D64 stable mean |
|---|---:|
| Gradient dense, unchanged sham | 6.25% |
| Gradient top-one | 47.27% |
| Hebbian dense | 6.25% |
| Hebbian top-one | 43.75% |

Frozen top-one access gains 41.02 points [32.03,50.00] for the gradient rule and 37.50 points [29.69,45.70] for Hebbian. Hebbian minus gradient under top-one is −3.52 points [−7.81,0.78], inconclusive for a rule difference in this transfer setting. These are not independently optimized Hebbian models. Every fork passes the declared short/K8 gates, though Hebbian top-one revision is lower (118/128 and121/128) than the original parents.

The unchanged gradient/dense fork reproduces all **7,296 common saved score vectors exactly** across the two parents, including short controls and online K8 D0/D64. This is a useful sham-reload/zero-trace invariant; it does not turn the other forks into a training reproduction.

## Initializers, learned dynamics and sleep timing

Exact untrained initializers were retained. K8,D64 online stable means before→after training are gradient dense 6.64→6.25%, gradient top-one 24.61→36.72%, Hebbian dense 6.64→5.47%, and Hebbian top-one 24.22→21.48%. The fixed-two-seed changes are respectively −0.39 [−4.30,3.52], +12.11 [4.29,20.31], −1.17 [−4.30,1.56], and −2.73 [−8.20,2.73] percentage points. Training provides a measured late-accuracy improvement here for gradient top-one, but no established late improvement for Hebbian top-one. See `fixed_seed_initial_final.csv`; short-task fitting and late retention must stay distinct.

Learned eta is approximately0.072 for trained gradient/dense,0.112 for gradient/top-one,0.082 for Hebbian/dense and0.130 for Hebbian/top-one. These independently learned values couple update strength and active-trace decay. The forks keep the parent eta and all representations fixed. Inactive-trace protection is guaranteed by the implementation, but no eta-only or read-only/write-only ablation was performed, so do not assign the gain to one unmeasured causal submechanism.

All recorded **final query states** have zero trace-saturation fraction. This does not inspect every transient training/online state and cannot establish that clipping never occurred. Module occupancy is independently reconstructed from the literal key-only router. Instrumented batch wall times include controller, validation, serialization and Python overhead; they are not bare-kernel latency benchmarks.

The sleep wrapper is exercised for online, repeated-current, continuous and periodic replay across all trained systems and neural anchors. It emits the same causal record plan as the frozen reference scheduler and resumes combined model/policy state at prefill and inside a delay block. The fixed-two-seed K8,D64 stable timing results are:

All intervals include or touch zero. Nothing here establishes a periodic sleep-timing advantage. The online anchor has fewer writes and is a separate contrast. Replay content, trace leakage, access and timing remain separate potential interventions. Full precision and other anchor contrasts are in `fixed_seed_timing.csv`.

GPU scores reproduce exactly; CPU raw-score error is at most9.54e−7 under the declared1e−5 tolerance, with every argmax and saved prediction matching. Both checks include actual combined-session continuation and slow/RNG/read-purity guards. These are selected inference checks, not retraining or complete-grid numerical reproduction. The prior pending GPU check passed and its old allocation ended at its one-hour limit; this pilot's allocation was observed cancelled after16m50s. Scheduler ownership/closure records belong to the coordinator.

All64 key IDs and atomic key/value bindings are familiar. Whole ordered training histories are excluded, not local subsets or atomic bindings. Full language encoding, complete Titans/MAC integration, learned routing, biologically faithful plasticity and teammate-final methods remain outside this component evidence. A suitable next experiment is fresh confirmation of selective access with a fixed declared comparison, then an eta/read/write-routing ablation; a Hebbian superiority or sleep-timing claim is unsupported by this pilot.
